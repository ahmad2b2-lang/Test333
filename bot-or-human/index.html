Signs of a bot, anywhere online

  • Lots of replies, very fast, at any hour
  • Personal stories with no real details
  • Agrees or praises but adds nothing new
  • Too tidy, too balanced, too polite
  • Answers the topic, not your exact words
  • Several accounts pushing the same idea
